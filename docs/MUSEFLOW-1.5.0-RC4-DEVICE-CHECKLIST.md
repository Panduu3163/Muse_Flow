# MuseFlow 1.5.0 RC4 device checks

Install the beta over RC3 without clearing data (version code 26).

1. Select Glow animated in Player settings. Open full Player and watch the larger lights move for at least 15 seconds; compare with the Mini player, whose subtler movement should remain unchanged. Check Light, Dark and AMOLED themes.
2. Watch the device gesture or three-button navigation area while opening and closing full Player and while using Next, Previous and queue selection. It should match the player background from the first frame without a black strip. Check Glow, Live mesh, Album gradient and Apple Music inspired styles. On three-button navigation, buttons must remain legible.
3. Select Apple Music inspired. The current cover should appear clear near the top and dissolve into a soft blurred backdrop below. Change tracks; verify old and new covers crossfade, and buttons, title, lyrics and queue remain readable. Try a track without artwork.
4. In Player settings, verify Background style is first, followed by the live preview, Artwork options and Progress and controls. The prior quick presets remain below those controls. Hide artwork, crop to square, rotate artwork, choose each progress style and reset; relaunch to check persistence.
5. With Rotating artwork on, play a song and confirm the circular cover turns slowly. Pause and resume: it should stop and continue smoothly. Android reduced motion should stop it. Check the preview as well.
6. Swipe the full-player cover left and right. It should move and shrink with your finger, then the next or previous cover should enter from the matching side and grow to size. Check Next/Previous buttons, rapid skipping and a repeated cover. Vertical volume swipe should still work.
7. Select Slim progress. It should be a thin line without a resting thumb, but tapping, dragging and TalkBack seeking should still work. Check Wavy and Squiggly play/pause motion and the picker previews.
